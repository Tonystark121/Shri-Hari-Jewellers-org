Transaction record trigger: 

"Before save :" the voucher check runs, and the system resets and stamps Gift Voucher, Voucher Discount, Points Redeemed and Net Amount.
"After save :" the voucher is marked Redeemed.
After save: loyalty points are redeemed and earned.
After save: the Account's Last 12 Month Spend is recalculated, which moves the tier.


GiftVoucherRedemption: 

Decides whether a voucher can be used on a bill, and calculates the discount.

Rejects the bill when:
both Redeem Points and Redeem Gift Voucher are ticked;
the voucher code or redemption channel is missing;
the code is unknown, or the voucher belongs to another customer;
the voucher is not Active, or it expired before the bill date;
the voucher has no offer type, value or Maximum Amount;
the bill is outside the voucher's amount range;
the same voucher is used twice in one save.

ShreeHariTrailingSpendBatch
The nightly recalculation of Last 12 Month Spend for every Account. It is needed so tiers drop when old bills age out of the window.

LoyaltyPointsExpiryBatch
The nightly job that marks expired lots as Expired and reduces the balance by the unspent points.

DynamicFieldController
The server side of the filter screen in the Create Gift Voucher flow.

Search: it turns the user's on-screen rules into a safe search (up to 2,000 records, respecting the user's access), including birthday and anniversary filters by month or next N days.
Voucher codes: it generates unique codes in the form PREFIX-XXXX.

ShreeHariLeadRestResource
The website's endpoint for creating Leads.

Validates:
last name and lead source are required;
at least one of phone or email is required;
picklist values, email format and field lengths;
dates must be real and not in the future.
Defaults: Company "Halsa Global", Status "New", Business Type "Unknown".
Routing: it resolves the store code and campaign name, and applies the Lead assignment rule.
Duplicates: a blocked duplicate Lead returns a 409. It does not check against existing customers.
