ShreeHari CRM - Questions for the client
========================================
Last updated: 15 Oct 2026

Background
----------
POS decides every redemption at the till. Salesforce is the back end: it
records what POS reports on each bill (points redeemed, gift voucher used)
and keeps the customer's loyalty points up to date. The points below are
cases where the two systems could disagree. Each one shows what Salesforce
does today, until the client decides.


1. POS reports more points redeemed than the customer has in Salesforce
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Example: POS says the customer redeemed 500 points; Salesforce shows only 300
usable points (possible after a sync delay, a manual correction, or points
that expired in Salesforce but not in POS).

Today: the bill is rejected with the message
  "The bill was not saved: Points Redeemed (500) is more than the points this
   customer can use on <date> (300)."

What should happen?
  a. Keep rejecting the bill (POS must correct it and send it again).
  b. Save the bill, deduct the 300 available, and flag the difference for
     someone to review.
  c. Save the bill as reported and let the balance go below zero.


2. The voucher is Expired or Cancelled in Salesforce, but POS accepted it
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Today: the bill is rejected ("voucher X expired on <date>" / "voucher X is
Cancelled in Salesforce").

What should happen?
  a. Keep rejecting.
  b. Save the bill and mark the voucher Redeemed anyway, as POS accepted it.


3. The voucher details from POS differ from the voucher in Salesforce
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Example: Salesforce has the voucher as Rs. 500 Fixed Price; POS reports
Rs. 300 off, or a Percentage offer, or a bill outside the voucher's
minimum/maximum amount.

Today: the bill is saved with POS's figures (offer type, face value,
discount); the voucher in Salesforce is marked Redeemed. Nothing is flagged.

Is that right, or should a difference be rejected / flagged for review?


4. Points and a gift voucher on the same bill
---------------------------------------------
Today: allowed - both are recorded (the old "one redemption per bill" rule
was removed because POS decides).

Can POS really do both on one bill, or should Salesforce reject it?


5. Other information on the POS bill
------------------------------------
Is anything else on the POS bill needed in Salesforce, or does it change the
points earned? For example: discounts other than vouchers, GST, making
charges, exchange of old gold.

Today: only the bill amount, voucher and points are used. Points are earned
on Net Amount = Amount - Voucher Discount - Points Redeemed, calculated by
Salesforce.


6. Net Amount
-------------
Today: Salesforce calculates Net Amount (see 5).

Should POS send the net amount it charged instead, once the POS API is live?


7. Converted Lead screen - extra line
-------------------------------------
On a converted Lead, the Lead Stage screen shows:
  "✔ This Lead is converted
   The customer bought on <date>. Their bills and loyalty points are on the
   account."
followed by links to the Account and Contact.

Keep the second line, shorten it to the purchase date only, or remove it?


When we are updating a lead phone/email what happens then -> with same as existing account simply create an enquiry
   for now we are refusing the save, and telling to create new enquiry, similar in case of duplicate lead.

Order parts Details:


I've mapped each of the 44 Shopify order properties to a Salesforce field, or marked it as staying on the staging record. A Shopify order lands in four places in your org:

Shopify part	Salesforce object	Count
Who bought	Account (household) and Contact (the person)	one
The purchase	Purchase Record (Transaction_Record__c, the bill)	one per order
What was bought	Purchase Line Item (Purchase_Line_Item__c), linked to Product2	one per entry in line_items
Everything else	stays on Staging Order only	—
Money: every Shopify amount is in paise, so divide by 100 before writing it to a Currency field (49293 becomes ₹492.93).

1. Customer: who placed the order → Account and Contact
Shopify order property	Where it goes	How
customer.id	Staging Customer Id__c	Links the order to the staging customer. Account has no Shopify ID field, so it can't be matched on directly.
phone, then customer.phone	Used to find the Account	Find the household by phone, then WhatsApp number, then email. This is the same rule Lead conversion uses in LeadConversionTransactionService.
email, then customer.email	Account Email__c / Contact Email	Fallback match, and fills the field if it's blank
customer.first_name / last_name	Contact FirstName / LastName	Only when a new Contact is created
billing_address	Account BillingAddress	address1 + address2 → Street, city → City, province → State, zip → PostalCode, country → Country
shipping_address	Account ShippingAddress / Contact MailingAddress	Same sub-fields as above
2. Purchase: the order itself → Purchase Record
Shopify order property	Purchase Record field	How
name (e.g. "#1001")	Bill_Number__c	Required and unique
id	POS_Reference_ID__c	Source reference for reconciliation
created_at	Transaction_Date__c	Required. Convert from "2022-04-29 11:15:46 -0400" to a DateTime.
total_price ÷ 100	Amount__c	Required. Already after discounts, before refunds.
transactions[] (gateway, kind = sale/capture, status = success)	Payment_Mode__c (Cash / Card / UPI / Wallet / Split)	Needs a gateway → value table. More than one successful payment means Split.
discount_applications[] where type = discount_code	Voucher_Code__c (code = title), Voucher_Discount__c (total_allocated_amount ÷ 100), Voucher_Redemption_Channel__c = Shopify	Only when the code is a Shree Hari Gift Voucher (see decision 4)
financial_status, cancelled, cancelled_at, cancel_reason	No field. They decide whether to create the bill at all.	See decision 2
Taken from the line items	Metal_Type__c	Gold, Silver or Platinum when every line is the same metal, Mixed otherwise
Taken from the line items	SKU_Details__c	Text summary of the lines
No Shopify property	Store__c, Points_Redeemed__c	See decisions 5 and 6
Loyalty points and the account's lifetime value (CLV), last-12-months spend and tier update by themselves once the bill is saved. The existing trigger and LoyaltyEngine handle that.

3. Products: what was bought → Purchase Line Item and Product2
Create one Purchase Line Item for each entry in line_items[]:

Shopify line_item property	Purchase Line Item field	How
sku	SKU__c, and Product__c	Look up Product2 where ProductCode = sku. That is the field the SKU Searcher uses.
title	Item_Description__c	
quantity	Quantity__c	
final_price ÷ 100	Unit_Price__c	Price of one piece after line discounts
final_line_price ÷ 100	Line_Amount__c	Equals final_price × quantity
grams	Weight_Grams__c	Shopify's shipping weight, which may not be the jewellery's net weight
product_id / variant_id	Staging Product Id__c	Product2 has no Shopify ID field
Product type, tags or metafield	Metal_Type__c	Not in line_items directly; read it from the product
Use line_items only. subtotal_line_items holds the same lines (minus tips), so using both would double-count.

4. Stays on Staging Order only (nothing to move)
These have no matching field in your data model. They remain on the staging record for reference:

Labels: cancel_reason_label, financial_status_label, fulfillment_status, fulfillment_status_label
Shopify identifiers and links: confirmation_number, order_number, customer_url, customer_order_url, order_status_url
Totals and price breakdown: item_count, line_items_subtotal_price, subtotal_price, shipping_price, tax_price, tax_lines, total_discounts, total_duties, total_refunded_amount, total_net_amount
Other order data: attributes, metafields, tags, note, shipping_methods, pickup_in_store?, cart_level_discount_applications, discounts (deprecated)
