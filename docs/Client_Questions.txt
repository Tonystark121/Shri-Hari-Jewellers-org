ShreeHari CRM - Questions for the client
========================================
Last updated: 15 Oct 2026

Background
----------
POS decides every redemption at the till. Salesforce is the back end: it
records what POS reports on each bill (points redeemed, gift voucher used)
and keeps the customer's loyalty points up to date. The points below are
cases where the two systems could disagree. Each one shows what Salesforce
does today, until the client decides.


1. POS reports more points redeemed than the customer has in Salesforce
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Example: POS says the customer redeemed 500 points; Salesforce shows only 300
usable points (possible after a sync delay, a manual correction, or points
that expired in Salesforce but not in POS).

Today: the bill is rejected with the message
  "The bill was not saved: Points Redeemed (500) is more than the points this
   customer can use on <date> (300)."

What should happen?
  a. Keep rejecting the bill (POS must correct it and send it again).
  b. Save the bill, deduct the 300 available, and flag the difference for
     someone to review.
  c. Save the bill as reported and let the balance go below zero.


2. The voucher is Expired or Cancelled in Salesforce, but POS accepted it
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Today: the bill is rejected ("voucher X expired on <date>" / "voucher X is
Cancelled in Salesforce").

What should happen?
  a. Keep rejecting.
  b. Save the bill and mark the voucher Redeemed anyway, as POS accepted it.


3. The voucher details from POS differ from the voucher in Salesforce
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Example: Salesforce has the voucher as Rs. 500 Fixed Price; POS reports
Rs. 300 off, or a Percentage offer, or a bill outside the voucher's
minimum/maximum amount.

Today: the bill is saved with POS's figures (offer type, face value,
discount); the voucher in Salesforce is marked Redeemed. Nothing is flagged.

Is that right, or should a difference be rejected / flagged for review?


4. Points and a gift voucher on the same bill
---------------------------------------------
Today: allowed - both are recorded (the old "one redemption per bill" rule
was removed because POS decides).

Can POS really do both on one bill, or should Salesforce reject it?


5. Other information on the POS bill
------------------------------------
Is anything else on the POS bill needed in Salesforce, or does it change the
points earned? For example: discounts other than vouchers, GST, making
charges, exchange of old gold.

Today: only the bill amount, voucher and points are used. Points are earned
on Net Amount = Amount - Voucher Discount - Points Redeemed, calculated by
Salesforce.


6. Net Amount
-------------
Today: Salesforce calculates Net Amount (see 5).

Should POS send the net amount it charged instead, once the POS API is live?


7. Converted Lead screen - extra line
-------------------------------------
On a converted Lead, the Lead Stage screen shows:
  "✔ This Lead is converted
   The customer bought on <date>. Their bills and loyalty points are on the
   account."
followed by links to the Account and Contact.

Keep the second line, shorten it to the purchase date only, or remove it?
